ListingCheck

Catches restaurant closures and changed hours before a diner makes a wasted trip. Every night it reads what restaurants post, the AI pulls out the dates with the exact words as evidence, fixed rules decide what may change, and the restaurant confirms with one tap.

The rules

The AI only reads. These fixed rules decide, based on who said it and how clear the evidence is. A changed listing is never marked "verified open".

EvidenceWhat happens
The restaurant's own authorised account, this outlet, written dates, posted recently, not cancelled by a later post, quote found in the noticeApplied Temporary closure added to the listing as "Detected from the restaurant's published notice". Restaurant notified and can undo. Booked diners told it is being confirmed.
A closure from anyone else, or a permanent closureUrgent Flagged and the restaurant is asked to confirm. Listing unchanged until then.
A change to regular hoursProposed Waits for the restaurant's one tap.
One diner reportCheck Triggers an early check with the restaurant. Never treated as proof.
Unclear outlet, unclear dates, or conflicting postsQuestion The restaurant is asked. Nothing changes.
A temporary closure reaches its end dateEnded Back to regular hours, shown as not separately verified.
